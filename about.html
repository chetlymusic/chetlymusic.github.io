About Chetly

Alternative Rock • Southlake, TX

Who We Are

Chetly is an alternative rock band based in Southlake, Texas. The band blends melody, energy, and character-driven songs into a sound that leans classic in spirit but modern in presentation.

This site is the central home for releases, demos, videos, and updates. New material may appear on streaming platforms, social pages, or private preview pages before wider release.

What We’re Doing

Chetly is actively writing, recording, and refining new music. Some songs are released publicly, while others remain in progress as demos, alternate mixes, or private works-in-progress.

Check the links below for the latest public music and updates.
