About Chetly

A little rock & roll, a little reflection, and a lot of life in between. Eleven songs. Years in the making.

Who We Are

Chetly is an alternative rock band based in Southlake, Texas. We blend melody, energy, and character-driven songwriting into music that feels classic in spirit and personal in perspective.

Our self-titled debut album was released October 30, 2026. Its eleven tracks bring together new material, fresh recordings of earlier songs, and bonus tracks shaped by years of collaboration.
