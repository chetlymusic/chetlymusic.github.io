About Chetly

Alternative rock · Southlake, Texas

We're Chetly — Jack Pier, Dave Jobes, Vic Ramirez, and Jon Beezley. We make guitar-driven music rooted in melody, stories, and the joy of playing together.

The debut album is finished.

After writing, recording, mixing, and mastering, our eleven-song debut album, Chetly, is complete. We're excited to finally share it.

Our release rollout begins with “Disappointed Citizen” on October 16, 2026, followed by “She Is the Water” on October 23. The full album arrives October 30, 2026.

Who we are

Based in Southlake, Texas, Chetly brings together a love of alternative rock, classic songwriting, and a collaborative approach to recording. The songs range from energetic rock & roll to more reflective moments — all part of the same record.

Jack Pier — vocals, guitar, keyboards
Dave Jobes — vocals, bass guitar, guitar, keyboards
Vic Ramirez — guitar
Jon Beezley — drums

What's in a name?

Chetly was the nickname of Jack's father, Chester. The album artwork includes Chester and Shirley — a small piece of family history that inspired the name. The music, though, is very much the work of the band you see above.
